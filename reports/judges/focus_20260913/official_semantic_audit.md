# Official profile semantic audit

The independent 40-observation sample passes after the reported fixes in builder **1.0.2**. The final package has **1,978 source observations and 15,171 cited facts**. No cross-person biography, court, county or contact attribution error was found in the sample. This is sampled QA, not full semantic certification.

The sample covered all eight individual profiles, six Texas assignments, six Iowa cards, six West Virginia biography blocks, five Utah cards, five Wisconsin entries and four Alabama historical rows (25 source documents). Five additional named records were checked to resolve findings.

Initial findings were two real judges omitted by name parsing (Thomas J. Judge and Joffie C. Pittman III), a personal citizenship sentence mislabeled service, and four career-after-graduation statements mislabeled education. A follow-up caught overbroad fixes removing Guerrero's Immigration Justice Project service, Delapena's B.A./J.D. credentials and Salter's appellate-division service category. All were independently rechecked as resolved against the original source blocks and final facts.

Independent integrity checks passed for all **293 artifacts / 38,080,335 bytes**, **149 capture/derivative linkages**, **145 sources**, all final fact/profile references and CSV row counts. The 98 collapsed Arkansas groups have 254 extra locators with identical full row values; Texas county assignments remain distinct. Eleven Arkansas clerk rows, 16 Delaware commissioner rows, Cocke's named administrative assistant and vacancy labels remain excluded. Historical Alabama dates and literal Present labels remain distinct from a verified current roster.

Read `official_semantic_audit.json` for the exact sample, issue history, source evidence, hash receipts and limits. The initial snapshot is retained for the pre-fix evidence. Original files, the builder and delivery outputs were not modified by this audit; no network or credential access was used.
