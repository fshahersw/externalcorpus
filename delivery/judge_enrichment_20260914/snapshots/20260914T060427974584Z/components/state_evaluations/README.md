# Colorado judicial evaluations — 2024 ballot cycle

This package contains all **116 published judge evaluations for Colorado's November 2024 retention ballot**, with complete evaluation narratives, literal page evidence, 116 commission findings, 114 numeric commission vote tallies and 28 explicitly reported overall survey ratings. It contains 478 source-specific facts. The separate official statistical report independently confirms the 116-judge ballot census: 3 Supreme Court justices, 5 Court of Appeals judges, 51 district judges and 57 county judges.

The source is the Office of Judicial Performance Evaluation's compilation published by Colorado Legislative Council Staff. Original bytes, source and final URLs, capture timestamps, SHA-256 hashes, extracted text and individual PDF pages are preserved under `sources/judges/enrichment_20260914/state_evaluations/`. There are four successful content downloads and two PDFs totaling 135 pages. No identity matching was performed. Capture date is September 14, 2026; the records describe the **2024 evaluation cycle**, not verified current employment or performance.

Use `observations.jsonl` or `observations.csv` to find judges and their narratives. `facts.jsonl` preserves names, roles, court contexts, full narratives, and source-reported survey limitations. `analyses.jsonl` contains only judge-specific findings, vote tallies and overall ratings. `context_analyses.jsonl` contains six statewide cycle statistics with null judge linkage. `methodology.json` preserves the complete cycle-specific methodology on PDF page 9. `source_manifest.jsonl`, `section_boundaries.jsonl`, `validation.json`, `checksums.json` and `gaps.jsonl` provide provenance and verification.

Ratings are **scale points**, not percentages. `scale_maximum` is separate from respondent numerator and denominator, which remain null. A commission vote has `votes_for_finding` and `votes_against_finding`; `observed_votes_cast` is their explicitly labeled sum and excludes absent, recused and vacant members. It is not a survey sample size. The source's survey window is January 2023–February 2024, and commissions also considered midterm surveys. Findings combine surveys with courtroom observation, written opinions, self-evaluation, case management reports and interviews. Source warnings about low response rates remain attributed to the commission. These materials do not establish case win rates, motion success rates, or causal judge effects.

Coverage is complete for the published ballot cycle, **not** the 120 total evaluated officers or 130 eligible officers. The four off-ballot evaluations and individual survey microdata/appendices were not acquired. Other percentages, respondent counts and dimension ratings remain in the complete narratives; only explicit overall-rating patterns were numerically normalized. Two narratives have no normalized numeric commission vote pair. Current-cycle status is unresolved beyond the historical source.

Two publication inconsistencies are preserved. Page 59 names **Kim Soon Shopshire** in its heading and **Kim Soon Shropshire** in its narrative; the observation carries `identity_safety_flags: ["source_name_spelling_conflict"]`. The Eighteenth Judicial District's county list differs between page 7 and page 113. District judges' county assignments remain null; county-court records use the explicit court heading. No spelling or jurisdiction discrepancy was silently resolved.

The commission host's robots request returned HTTP 403 and that host was paused; no access restriction was bypassed. The legislature and State Publications Library supplied the public official copies through ordinary HTTP. Rebuild offline with `sources/judges/enrichment_20260914/state_evaluations/normalize_colorado_2024.py`. The validator checks source/page hashes, literal character slices, foreign keys, court-level and finding totals, negative-finding attribution and rating/denominator separation. Its output hash map binds validation to the exact JSONL files.

Primary source links: [Legislative publication](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2024-blue-book-judicial-performance-evaluations), [complete evaluation PDF](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/judicial_performance_evaluations-accessible.pdf), [official 2024 statistical report](https://spl.cde.state.co.us/artemis/scserials/sc220210internet/sc2202102024internet.pdf).
